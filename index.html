Why Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is one of the fastest-growing fields on the planet, with over 3.5 million unfilled jobs worldwide. Whether you want to protect hospitals from ransomware, hunt nation-state hackers, or build the next generation of secure software — it starts here.

AP Cybersecurity is a college-level course that earns you real academic credit while building skills the industry desperately needs. No experience required — just the willingness to think like an attacker to build like a defender.

The challenges above are a preview of how we learn: by doing. Every concept is paired with a hands-on activity. Welcome to the class where homework is hacking.
